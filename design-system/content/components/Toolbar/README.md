Toolbar groups the collection tools: search, filters, sort, refresh and paging, with ToolbarButton, ToolbarDivider and ToolbarGroup.

Compose actions by task and do not insert a field's business state. Changing the query resets the page number; switching view (ViewModeToggle) keeps search, filters and paging. Give the search a stable explicit width so its clear button does not move neighbouring controls, and let groups wrap on narrow screens without changing their order.

The consumer provides every action and its behaviour.
